# One group for every crawler. Per-bot groups used to exist for the AI
# crawlers; under RFC 9309 a bot that matches a specific group reads only
# that group, and those groups carried no Disallow lines, so they quietly
# lifted the admin and analytics exclusions for exactly those bots. Nothing
# here forbids AI crawlers: /api/events, /api/health and /llms.txt are open.
User-agent: *
Allow: /
Disallow: /api/admin
Disallow: /api/stats
Disallow: /api/analytics
Disallow: /api/push
Disallow: /admin

Sitemap: https://warlibrary.midatlantic.ai/sitemap.xml
